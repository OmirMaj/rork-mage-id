Straight answers

The questions every contractor asks.

Do I have to throw out the tools I use now?

No. Start with estimating and the learning engine — the part nobody else has. Keep what works, and let MAGE quietly take over pricing, job costing, and scheduling as it earns its place.

How does it actually "learn my costs"?

Every job you finish feeds your real numbers back into your catalog — automatically. No spreadsheets to maintain. Job #50 prices off 49 jobs of truth, not a generic price book.

Is my cost data private?

Yes. Your numbers are yours. The brain learns your business — it never hands your hard-won costs to the contractor down the street.

Do my subs really use it for free?

Yes, and so does your whole crew. Field seats — subs, foremen, anyone who works the job rather than the books — are unlimited and free on every plan including Free, permanently. Office seats, the ones that can see margin, are included two on Pro and five on Business. That is the whole rule; there is no other seat math and no per-seat fee at any tier. The seat allowance in full.

Does it work on my phone, on the jobsite?

It's built for the field first: price hidden conditions from a photo, build a schedule by voice, and update the job from the truck.

What does it cost to start?

Free to try, no card, on the web today. Pro is $29/mo and Business $79/mo — but they are not self-serve yet: our payment processor is still in sandbox and the iPhone app is in TestFlight rather than the App Store, so you email us and we switch the plan on, usually the same day.
