02 Estimates & AI

From PDF plans to signed estimate in minutes.

Drop a PDF of architect's drawings — the Gemini Vision-powered analyzer reads every page, identifies scope, measures quantities, and prices it in 30–60 seconds. Every line editable, every margin transparent, every correction trains the model on the next job.

  • AI Drawing Analyzer. PDF in, priced estimate out. Standard or Pro Estimator depth.
  • AI Quantity Takeoff. LF / SF / EA / CY measured from the sheet, ready to route into a buyout package.
  • Spec-Match. One tap to match callout codes against the spec book.
  • Field verification. Walk the site, verify or correct each AI row — corrections feed back into accuracy.
