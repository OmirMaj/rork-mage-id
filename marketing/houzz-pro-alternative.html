Is MAGE ID cheaper than Houzz Pro?

MAGE is a flat $29–$79/mo with no per-seat fees — unlimited free field seats, and two office seats on Pro, five on Business, and no paid-lead or ad upsell. The seat rule in full. We cannot tell you what Houzz Pro will cost you. Check Houzz Pro’s own site for its current price and compare the two.

Does MAGE generate a schedule from an estimate?

Yes. It builds a full CPM schedule with critical path and Last Planner, with weather-aware reflow shipped. On a day we cannot get a live forecast the app says so and refuses to write a delay record from a guess.
