Frequently asked.

If we missed yours, the support page has a longer list.

How accurate is the AI takeoff?
Accuracy depends on the drawing. Clean architectural sets with visible dimensions and schedules return high-confidence numbers on most rooms, walls, doors, and windows. Hand-sketched plans or scans without dimension strings get medium-to-low confidence and the AI flags them in the Concerns section. Every quantity is editable. We publish per-category accuracy from anonymized corrections data and the Corrections panel on every project shows you exactly where the AI was off — so you can spot patterns before bidding.
What types of plans does it work on?
Architectural floor plans, elevations, sections, RCPs, schedules (door / window / finish / wall), MEP plans, and site plans. Single-family and multi-family residential are the primary target. Light commercial works. Heavy industrial / structural with deep MEP coordination is on the roadmap.
Can I export quantities to Excel?
Today CSV and PDF export are supported. Native Excel with formulas is on the near roadmap. If you need it before we ship, the CSV opens cleanly in Excel and Google Sheets.
Does it run offline?
The takeoff itself needs cloud AI to run. But once the takeoff lands on your phone, you can view, edit, and capture field-verification photos offline — everything queues and syncs the moment you're back on signal. Daily reports, change orders, punch items, photos, voice captures all work fully offline.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Read-only access to everything you've created stays available for 90 days after cancel. Full export of all projects, estimates, photos, daily reports, and PDFs at any time, no cancellation required.
Is there a free trial?
Free tier with one active project, no time limit. Pro and Business unlock everything else — start free on the web at app.mageid.app, cancel anytime.
Do you integrate with QuickBooks?
In active development. Today MAGE ID handles the entire invoice / change-order / AIA pay-app flow in-app, and exports a clean CSV for your bookkeeper. If QuickBooks sync is a hard blocker for switching, let us know — demand drives priority.
Do you have an Android app?
Yes. iOS, Android, and web all share the same codebase. Sign in once, use any of them.
What's the price?
Pro is $29/month or $288/year (about 2 months free). Business is $79/month or $792/year. Both replace a stack of point tools that typically runs $400–$700/month. See the full breakdown on the Compare page.
