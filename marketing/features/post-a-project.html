New · Homeowner & Owner Marketplace

Post a project. Contractors who cover your area bid on it.

Whether it’s a kitchen remodel or a 50-unit residential block, post your scope, drawings, and photos in five minutes. MAGE ID alerts contractors whose service area covers your project and shows you how many it reached — including none, if nobody covers your area yet. You pick a winner, they get a project in their MAGE ID account with your details, and they send you the link to your project portal.

How it works.

Four steps. Your post shows how many contractors it reached, so you’re never left guessing.

①

Post your project

Title, scope, address, budget range, photos of the property, optional drawings or sketches.

②

Contractors who cover your area are alerted

MAGE ID alerts contractors whose service area covers your project, and your post shows how many it reached. Contractors can’t browse open posts yet, so a post outside every service area reaches no one — and says so.

③

Review bids side-by-side

Every bid in one place. Shortlist favorites and compare them side-by-side.

④

Award — the contractor sends your portal link

One tap to award. The contractor gets a project in their MAGE ID account with what you posted and your contact details. Once they’ve set up your project portal — messaging, change orders, invoices, photos, daily reports — they send you the link.

For contractors: real leads, no scraping.

MAGE ID alerts contractors whose service area covers a homeowner’s project. Service-area setup is coming — until it’s live, and until browsing open posts opens, posts won’t reach you. When one does, you can bid on it or request a site visit before quoting if you can’t price it sight-unseen.

  • Real homeowner posts, with photos and scope — not scraped listings
  • Alerts for projects in your service area, once service-area setup is available
  • Submit estimate or request a site visit first — your call
  • Win the bid? The project is created in your MAGE ID account with the homeowner’s drawings, photos, scope and contact details — you set up their portal and send them the link
  • Free to bid — we make money when you upgrade for the rest of the OS

Built so the right people show up.

Spam and trolls kill marketplaces. We design against them.

Address verification

Every post is geocoded. Bad addresses get flagged before contractors waste time.

Photo-required posts

At least one photo of the property. You’ve been there. The bots haven’t.

Contractor profiles

Contractors can list certifications, bond capacity, and completed projects on their profile.

Bond & license fields

Filter by required certs (MWBE, SBA-8a, SDVOSB, state-specific). Big jobs only see qualified bidders.

No fees on award

Money flows direct between you and your contractor. We don’t take a cut of project value.

The award starts the project.

Most platforms hand you a phone number and disappear. When you award on MAGE ID:

  • The contractor is notified
  • A new project is created in their MAGE ID account — drawings, photos, scope, your contact details and the awarded price carried over
  • They set up your client portal — messaging, document exchange, change-order approvals, progress photos — and send you the link
  • Other bidders are politely declined automatically
  • Your project is now in flight — not in a long email thread
