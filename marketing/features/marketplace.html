Local supplier directory.

A curated directory of construction suppliers — lumber yards, steel, concrete, masonry, MEP — across nine US metros. Refreshed weekly. Filter by material type, distance, and delivery options.

  • Indexed metros: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Dallas, Phoenix, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Miami
  • Filter by material category (lumber, concrete, steel, finishes, MEP, tools)
  • Distance-sorted with drive time estimates
  • Delivery availability and lead times
  • One-tap call, navigate, or open website

Material prices that become yours.

Day one, materials price off a regional cost index — ten regional factors and twenty metro adjustments, so a Bay Area bid is not priced off a Tulsa number. It is a starting point and the app says so on the screen. From your first scanned receipt it is your pricing: snap a supply-house invoice and those unit prices go straight into a price book only you can see, the same day. No more “pricing from memory” off a 2022 job, and no pretending a national average is what your yard charges you.

  • Regional cost factors on day one, clearly labelled as a starting point
  • Common-material catalog: lumber grades, rebar, concrete, drywall, pipe, conductor
  • Scanned supplier invoices write real unit prices into your own book, same day
  • Pull directly into estimate line items
  • Your own price history per item as the receipts accumulate
  • Regional price variance flagged (when your zip is 20%+ above average)

Equipment — owned and rented, tracked.

Every machine in one list, whether you own it or rented it for the week. See each piece's daily rate, whether it's available, in use, or down for maintenance, and which job it's on — so nothing sits idle or slips past a service date.

  • Owned + rented equipment in one fleet view
  • Daily rate on every machine
  • Status at a glance: available, in use, maintenance
  • See which job each piece is assigned to
  • Maintenance schedule with overdue-service flags
  • Fleet totals: how many pieces, how many in use
