The four moves no other tool ships.

These are MAGE ID's real differentiators in the takeoff lane — features that require the rest of the contractor OS to even make sense.

Spec book matcher

PT-1 → Sherwin Williams Eggshell SW7036

Upload the architect's spec PDF and the AI pairs every callout code on the drawings with the actual manufacturer, product, finish, and SKU. Unmatched codes get flagged as RFIs before you bid them.

Takeoff → Buyout

One tap. Eight to twelve sub-trade packages.

The takeoff turns into draft buyout packages — concrete, framing, doors, drywall, plumbing, electrical, HVAC — with auto-written scope text including quantities and matched specs. A Friday-afternoon job, in 60 seconds.

Field verification

AI vs. field, side by side.

Tap a row on site, snap a photo with your iPhone, type what you measured. GPS attaches automatically. Green if within 5%, yellow up to 15%, red beyond. Patterns surface in the corrections panel.

Per-row confidence + source pages

Trace any number to where it came from.

Every quantity carries a high / medium / low confidence rating with strict rules. Tap the source-page badge to open Page Inspector — see the rendered drawing with a sidebar of every other quantity from that page.
