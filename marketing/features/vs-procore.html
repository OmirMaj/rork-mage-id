When Procore is the right call

We genuinely respect Procore — for the right customer. If you're running a $100M+ commercial GC with a project-engineer pool, dedicated VDC team, and 100+ active projects, Procore's depth on drawings, BIM coordination, prequalification workflows, and equity-grade audit trails is real. You can afford the cost and you'll use the depth.

You should pick Procore if:

  • Your annual volume exceeds $50M and most projects are commercial GMP/CM
  • You have a project engineer or two per active job
  • BIM coordination, drawing markup, and submittal logs are daily workflows for your team
  • Your clients/owners specifically require Procore (some institutional owners do)

You should pick MAGE ID if:

  • You're a residential GC, custom home builder, design-build firm, or small-commercial contractor
  • Annual volume is $500K–$30M
  • You're a hands-on owner — the PM software has to live in your truck, not on a project engineer's desktop
  • You want AI estimating + AI takeoff + AI permit research in the same app, not three separate vendors
  • $10,000/year of PM software cost is your single biggest line-item after labor
