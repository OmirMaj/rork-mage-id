The first contractor app with an AI agent that quotes actual building, zoning, energy, and local-law code text — with verbatim quotes and section citations. Trained on 172 hand-curated sections across 8 major US construction markets. No hallucinations. No "ask your expediter."

"Can I extend my roof in Brooklyn?"

Type the question the way you'd ask a colleague. The agent loads the relevant code sections for your selected metro, quotes them with section numbers and links to the primary .gov source, and gives you a 2–4 paragraph answer grounded in the text.

  • Verbatim quotes. Real code text, not paraphrased.
  • Section citations. Every answer cites NYC BC §1020.2 or ZR §24-522 or whatever applies.
  • Source URLs. Tap to open the .gov page directly.
  • Hedged when uncertain. The agent says "verify with [authority]" instead of inventing.

What it isn't — and the boundaries we publish.

No tool is more credible than the one that tells you its limits up front. The Permit Q&A agent is a research aid for small GCs, not a substitute for a licensed expediter or attorney. We hedge when uncertain. We never invent timelines or fees. About a third of the corpus is flagged "(synthesized)" — meaning the substance is accurate per cross-checked official sources, but the wording was reconstructed because the source PDF was binary-blocked or the live page was 403'd.

  • Not legal advice. Always verify against primary text for litigation use.
  • ICC base codes excluded. IBC/IRC/IECC/IFC/IPC/IMC base text is ICC copyright. We quote only when adopted-by-reference into local law, and we cite the local jurisdiction.
  • Quarterly refresh. Codes amend regularly. Our refresh cadence is per-metro spot-checks every quarter, full deep-dives annually.
  • Suggests an expediter when a question is contested, jurisdiction-specific, or filing-specific.
