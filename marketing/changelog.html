Every change, dated.

Last updated 11 September 2026

Nothing gets quietly removed from this page. If it goes quiet for a month, that tells you something, and it should — that is exactly why it is here. Dates are the day the work landed in the codebase, not the day we felt like announcing it.

Building now

No dates promised. These are the things actually being worked on, which is a different and much shorter list than the things we would like to build.

  • DCMA #6 High Float and #12 Critical Path Test — the two remaining checks that are actually computable here. Ten of the fourteen run in full today and an eleventh runs as a labelled proxy. #14 BEI is not on this list and will not be: it needs a data date the engine deliberately does not have, and we would rather run twelve honestly than fourteen with one faked.
  • A working till. The paid plans are switched on by hand right now because our payment processor is still in sandbox and the iPhone app is in TestFlight rather than the App Store. Both are in progress. Until they are done the pricing page says so at the top.
  • Real screenshots of the pay application and the WIP schedule. Four feature pages carried empty “preview coming” boxes; those are gone rather than left promising an image, and the captures will be added when they exist.

Not doing

These are real needs and we are not the answer to them. If one of these is a dealbreaker, buy something else — we would rather you knew now than in month three.

  • Accounts payable. No payables ledger, no vendor aging, no pay-when-paid logic, no check run, no batch payment file. MAGE ID never moves money; “paid” means you paid it somewhere else and recorded how. Where exactly that line falls.
  • Certified payroll and Davis-Bacon. Not built and not planned.
  • QuickBooks Desktop and Sage. QuickBooks Online only. If your books live in Desktop, that is a fair reason to buy something else.
  • Multi-approver change-order routing chains. Change orders are approved, not routed through a hierarchy.
  • Buying reviews or badges. Why, and what we offer instead.

We don’t take feature requests through a form. Email help@mageid.app — it reaches the person who writes the code. If three contractors ask for the same thing, it moves.
