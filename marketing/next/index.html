    Where The Numbers Come From

    Your Prices, Not Ours

    A cost catalog somebody else wrote does not know what you pay your subs. MAGE ID builds a price book from your own jobs. When you close a job, what you signed and paid your subs and suppliers for that work goes into the book.

    The book starts empty. You can type in the rates you already know, and they stay marked as rates you set until a closed job backs them up. When the assistant drafts an estimate, it uses the rates in your book that fit the work and falls back to market averages for the rest. The estimate tells you which it used, and you approve every line before it goes out.

    The price book and AI estimate drafts are on Pro. We do not publish an accuracy number. There are not enough closed jobs yet to earn one.
