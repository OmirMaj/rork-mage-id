Pricing questions, answered.

Are subcontractors free?

Yes. Subs you invite to a project never pay. They get the sub portal — daily progress, schedule, photos, COIs, prequal, RFIs — for free, on the GC's plan.

Are clients (homeowners / owners) free?

Yes. Homeowners get the client portal — schedule, selections, daily updates, contract, payments — without ever creating an account. Just a magic link.

Are there per-project fees?

No. Pro is unlimited projects. Most enterprise platforms charge $50–200 per active project; we don't.

What does "Free forever" actually cover?

The Free plan is free forever. One active project, daily reports, a basic schedule, and a few free AI trials — so you can run a job before you pay. The live client portal is a Pro feature.

Is there an annual discount?

Yes — every paid tier has an annual plan, and Enterprise’s discount is the steepest.

What we are not doing on this page is printing a yearly dollar figure, and you should know why rather than assume we forgot. Four different annual numbers were circulating across five pages of this site until September 2026. Not one of them came from the store — they came from people doing arithmetic on different pages. The figure that belongs here is whatever the store product actually charges, and that listing is not live yet (see the note above the prices: we cannot take your card at all today). When it is, the number goes in this one place and nowhere else, so it cannot drift again.

Until then the monthly rate is the whole price list — Free, Pro $29, Business $79, Enterprise $150, per company, per month — and the app shows you the exact amount before you confirm anything.

Month-to-month at $29 comes to $348 over a year if you never switch to annual; where another page quotes that figure it says so, because a month-to-month total printed as if it were the annual price is the kind of small dishonesty that makes a buyer re-check every other number on the site.

What's the difference between Business and Enterprise?

Same feature set. Higher AI quotas. Business already includes the full stack — Cost X-Ray, the Bid Advisor, Ask Your Plans, Track Record, the margin engine, safety management, WIP reporting, and crew/worker profiles on top of everything in Pro. Business is sized for a typical multi-office GC (80 daily AI requests, 50 drawing analyses/mo). Enterprise nearly doubles those caps for teams running AI-heavy workflows — drawing analyses, photo triage, spec extraction at scale. If you're hitting Business caps two months in a row, Enterprise will pay for itself in time saved.

Does it do accounts payable?

No — and here is exactly where the line is, because a bare “no” would mislead you in both directions.

What it does do. Photograph a sub’s paper bill or a supply-house invoice and it is read, checked with you, and posted as a cost against the right job and the right cost code (that is Scan Anything, on Business). Subs you have set up with a portal submit their invoices to you directly, and you approve or reject with notes. Bills entered in QuickBooks Online land in a review queue here and you approve them line by line against the job, rather than having them appear silently in your costs. When you pay, you record how — check number, ACH trace, card — so paid-versus-owed ties out to your bank statement and your 1099s are real. Mark something paid without that detail and the app flags it as unreconciled instead of quietly closing the balance.

What it does not do. There is no payables ledger. No vendor aging report, no pay-when-paid logic, no check run, no batch payment file, and no way to originate a bill on the GC side that a sub did not submit. MAGE ID never moves money — “paid” here means you paid it somewhere else and told us.

So: if you need real AP, keep QuickBooks and connect it, or buy something built for AP. If what you actually need is “every cost landed on the right job before I bill for it,” that is what this is for.

Can I switch between tiers?

Up or down anytime. Downgrade to Free and Pro features become read-only on existing data — nothing is deleted, you can re-upgrade later.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Nothing is deleted. Paid features become read-only on your existing data, and everything you have created stays viewable and exportable. You can export the full archive — every project with its estimate and revision history, plus invoices, change orders, daily reports, punch items, photos, RFIs, submittals, warranties, contacts and subcontractors — at any time, before or after cancelling, without asking us. Re-subscribe and everything picks up where it left off. We do not hold data hostage to force a retention call; there is nobody here to make one.

Try the export on day one, before you trust us with anything. Settings → Data Export, one tap, JSON or CSV, human-readable. It was built before the paywall was, deliberately — proving you can leave is the cheapest way to find out whether the rest of this page is worth believing. What moves in and what moves out.
