Pricing questions, answered.

Are subcontractors free?

Yes. Subs you invite to a project never pay. They get the sub portal — daily progress, schedule, photos, COIs, prequal, RFIs — for free, on the GC's plan.

Are clients (homeowners / owners) free?

Yes. Homeowners get the client portal — schedule, selections, daily updates, contract, payments — without ever creating an account. Just a magic link.

Are there per-project fees?

No. Pro is unlimited projects. Most enterprise platforms charge $50–200 per active project; we don't.

What does "Starter free forever" actually cover?

One active project, the full DFR + photo + basic schedule + manual estimate workflows, and the client portal for one homeowner. AI features (estimating, takeoff, copilot, code Q&A), public bids, the sub directory, and the EVM dashboard live in Pro.

Is there an annual discount?

Yes — and Enterprise has the steepest discount. Pro drops to ~$24/mo effective when billed annually (about 2 months free), Business drops to ~$66/mo (about 2 months free), and Enterprise drops to ~$83/mo effective when billed annually (over 5 months free, $999.99/yr vs. $149.99 × 12 = $1,799.88). Starter is free either way.

What's the difference between Business and Enterprise?

Same features. Higher AI quotas. Business is sized for a typical multi-office GC (80 daily AI requests, 50 drawing analyses/mo). Enterprise nearly doubles those caps for teams running AI-heavy workflows — drawing analyses, photo triage, spec extraction at scale. If you're hitting Business caps two months in a row, Enterprise will pay for itself in time saved.

Can I switch between tiers?

Up or down anytime. Downgrade to Starter and Pro features become read-only on existing data — nothing is deleted, you can re-upgrade later.

What about iOS / Android / web?

One subscription works across iOS, Android, and the web app at app.mageid.app. Sign in once anywhere.

Need something beyond Enterprise?

If you need volume pricing for 50+ seats, custom data residency, or a dedicated tenant — get in touch.

Refunds?

14-day free trial means you don't pay until day 15. After that, monthly plans don't refund partial months; annual plans get a prorated refund within 30 days.
