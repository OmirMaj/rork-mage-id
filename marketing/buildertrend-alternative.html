Questions contractors ask.

Is MAGE ID cheaper than Buildertrend?

Yes — a flat $29–$79/mo with no per-seat fees — unlimited free field seats, and two office seats on Pro, five on Business (the rule in full), vs. Buildertrend’s quote-based pricing, which buyers report runs several hundred dollars a month and climbs at renewal. Buildertrend does not charge per seat either — the difference here is that our number is published.

Can I import my projects?

Partly, and we are specific about which parts. Pipeline, contacts and subs paste across in minutes. Your price book is seeded from rates you already know — stored as a claim, not a measurement, so a seeded book still honestly reads “0 closed jobs”. Schedules import from .xlsx or MS Project XML. Historical job-cost detail from another platform does not come across, on purpose: a half-imported cost history quietly poisons the numbers your estimator learns from. Easiest path is to finish your live jobs where they are and run the next one here.

Does MAGE do scheduling like Buildertrend?

Yes — plus Last Planner pull planning (with weather-aware reflow shipped), which Buildertrend doesn’t have.

Is there a free version?

Yes — free for your first project, and subs are always free, with no per-seat fees.
