Questions contractors ask.

How is MAGE ID different from JobTread?

This page describes MAGE ID only. MAGE learns your real costs from finished jobs and prices the next bid off your own history, and flags margin erosion live. Check JobTread’s own site for what JobTread includes.

Is MAGE ID cheaper than JobTread?

MAGE is a flat $29–$79/mo with no per-seat fees — unlimited free field seats, and two office seats on Pro, five on Business, and subs free (the rule in full). We cannot tell you what JobTread will cost you. Check JobTread’s own pricing page with your real seat count and compare the two.

Does MAGE ID have scheduling?

Yes. MAGE has CPM scheduling with critical path and Last Planner pull planning, and ties the schedule to margin, with weather-aware reflow shipped. On a day we cannot get a live forecast the app says so and refuses to write a delay record from a guess.

Is there a free version of MAGE ID?

Yes — free for your first project, and subs are always free.
