1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (encrypted with bcrypt) when you sign up. Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In provide name + email from your existing account.
  • Profile information: business name, license number, insurance details (COI), trade specialties, address, phone number, and optionally public profile content (photos, descriptions, ratings).
  • Project content: project descriptions, drawings, schedules, estimates, invoices, change orders, daily field reports, RFIs, submittals, photos, voice transcripts, and related construction management content you create in the app.
  • Contacts: people you add to your contact directory (subcontractors, vendors, clients) — names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses.
  • Tax / financial: for 1099-NEC export, you may store the last 4 digits of a subcontractor's EIN or SSN. We do not collect or store full Social Security Numbers.
  • Payment information: handled by Stripe and RevenueCat — we do not store full credit card numbers. We receive only the last 4 digits, card brand, and expiration month for receipt display.
  • Communications: messages you send through the app, support requests, voice input you provide for transcription.
  • Lead submissions: if you submit a homeowner project through our marketing site form (mageid.app/lead.html), we collect your name, email, phone (optional), ZIP code, budget range, and project description.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: operating system, device model, app version, screen size, language, time zone.
  • Usage data: pseudonymous event-level analytics about which features you use, how often, and basic performance metrics. Tied to a randomly-generated user identifier, not to your name or email.
  • Diagnostic data: error reports and crash logs (scrubbed of personal content before submission).
  • Location data (with your permission): when you take a photo or use distance-based features, we capture GPS coordinates at the moment of capture. Location is not tracked in the background.
  • Camera, microphone, and photo library access (with your permission): only used when you actively invoke a feature that requires them (e.g., taking a jobsite photo, voice-capturing a daily report).

4. AI features and training

MAGE ID includes AI-powered features (permit Q&A, code check, AI takeoff, AI estimate review, photo analysis, drawing analysis, weekly snapshot). When you invoke an AI feature, the relevant prompt — and, where applicable, a specific file or photo — is sent to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic, Google, and/or OpenAI) for processing. The provider returns a response, which we display to you and store in your project.

Important:

  • We do not use your project data, photos, messages, or AI inputs/outputs to train our own machine-learning models.
  • Our third-party AI providers process AI requests under enterprise / API terms that prohibit using inputs and outputs to train their general models (Anthropic API, Google Gemini API, and OpenAI API terms all include this commitment for API use).
  • We do not send personally identifying information (names, email, contact details) to AI providers unless that information is intentionally part of the prompt (e.g., you ask the agent "draft an email to John" and include the name).
  • AI outputs are not guaranteed to be accurate. The Permit Q&A agent in particular is a research aid, not legal advice. Verify code and permit requirements with the appropriate authority before acting.

5. Data storage, residency, and security

Your project data lives in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase on AWS us-east-1. Here is exactly how it's protected:

  • In transit: TLS 1.2+ on every connection. No plaintext API endpoints exist.
  • At rest: AES-256 on the database volume and on every backup.
  • Passwords: bcrypt with a per-user salt. Nobody at MAGE ID — including us — can read your password.
  • Row-level access: Postgres Row Level Security policies block every query that tries to read a row owned by another user. This is enforced at the database, not in app code, so a bug in our backend cannot leak someone else's project to you.
  • Edge functions: stateless, ephemeral, isolated per invocation. They cannot read or write outside the request scope.
  • Backups: encrypted, daily, retained 7 days. We do not ship backups outside AWS.
Data residency note for non-US users: when you use MAGE ID, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with our sub-processors where required for international transfers.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain your account and project data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account). When you delete your account, all associated project data is permanently removed within 30 days from our primary database. Encrypted backups are purged within 90 days.

We may retain limited information after deletion to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements (for example: tax-related transaction records required for 7 years under US tax law).

7. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct — ask us to fix inaccurate information.
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Export — receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format (Settings → Data Export inside the app).
  • Object or restrict — limit certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app tools (Settings → Data Export, Settings → Account → Delete) or email privacy@mageid.app. We respond within 30 days (45 days under CCPA, extendable by 45 with notice).

Your California rights
