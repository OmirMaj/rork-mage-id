MAGE-AI mark — 8 fresh directions

You said the wand-+-"AI"-strip format was limiting. So I broke it open: 8 independent directions — emblems, the wand fused into a letter, badges, tools-as-letters, and letterless symbols — each scored by a design director on construction-feel, magic, 20px legibility, originality, and how well it flexes (app icon / tab / inline). Every mark is shown on light + dark, at real 20px, and as an app-icon tile.

DIRECTOR'S PICK

Gantry M

The only mark that does all three jobs at once: it is the brand (an M for MAGE), it reads as construction (a portal/gantry truss with steel-baseplate footings — not a typeface letter), and the amber spark is structural — the apex of the build, not a sparkle pasted on. Most ownable/trademark-distinct, best 20px legibility (one continuous beam path + a single accent), and it fills a square app icon, a tab glyph, and an inline slot without changing shape. Runner-up Spark Truss if you want a name-agnostic symbol.
