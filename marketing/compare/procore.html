Three things Procore won't do for you.

Not "can't afford to build." Won't — because their customer is the enterprise, and this is what the small GC actually needs to stay profitable.

01 · It learns

Your prices, not a catalog's

Every closed job teaches MAGE what things really cost you — your subs, your supply house, your market. The next bid is priced off your own history, so you stop leaving money on the table or pricing yourself out. Nobody at the enterprise tier does this.

02 · It guards

Margin defense, live

A risk score and an alert the week a job starts slipping — not a surprise at the final invoice. The whole point of MAGE is that you find out a kitchen reno is bleeding in week 3, while you can still do something about it.

03 · It reschedules

Around the weather, automatically

Rain hits your slab pour, MAGE pushes the affected work and cascades the schedule for you — and logs the delay day. Procore's AI tells you a delay is likely. MAGE moves the job. That's the difference between a warning and a tool.
