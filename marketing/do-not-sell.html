Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last updated: May 11, 2026

MAGE ID does not sell personal information. MAGE ID does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We never have.

The California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) requires us to give California residents a clearly-labeled link titled "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information." This page is that link. Because we don't engage in sales or cross-context sharing, there is nothing for you to opt out of.

What we do with your data

We collect personal information solely to operate the Service. We share it only with the operational sub-processors listed in our Privacy Policy, Section 3 (Supabase for hosting, Stripe for payment processing, AI providers for AI features, etc.), and each is contractually limited to using the data only on our instructions.

We do not:

  • Sell personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or any third party.
  • Share personal information with third parties for the purpose of cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Run third-party advertising trackers or behavioral cookies on our marketing site or in our apps.
  • Disclose your project data, photos, contact lists, or messages to anyone except as needed to provide the Service (e.g., delivering an email through Resend) or as required by law.

Other California rights

Even though there is no sale or sharing to opt out of, you have other California rights under CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Right to delete personal information we have about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information (e.g., precise geolocation, last-4-of-SSN we collect for 1099-NEC export).
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

See the California Residents section of our Privacy Policy for the full list and exact details.

How to make a request

To exercise any California right (or any equivalent right under another state's privacy law), email privacy@mageid.app with the subject "California Privacy Request" and include your name and the email address associated with your account.

We respond to verified requests within 45 days (extendable by 45 with notice). For deletion and access requests, we will first verify your identity using reasonable means proportionate to the sensitivity of the request.

You may also authorize an agent to make a request on your behalf; we will require written authorization signed by you, plus identity verification of both you and the agent.

Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor the Global Privacy Control signal sent by your browser as a valid opt-out preference signal where applicable. Because we don't sell or share personal information, receipt of a GPC signal does not change our handling — but we record the signal as evidence of your stated preference.
