What LL97 actually says

The thresholds. LL97 covers any NYC building over 25,000 sq ft, or two-plus buildings on the same tax lot totaling over 50,000 sq ft, or two-plus condo buildings under the same board totaling over 50,000 sq ft. Below those numbers, you're exempt.

The caps. They're set per occupancy group in kgCO2e per square foot per year, with two tiers: an easier 2024–2029 cap and a much tighter 2030–2034 cap. Most buildings will pass the first window and miss the second without electrification work — heat pumps, controls, on-site solar, or REC purchases (capped at 30% of obligation).

The penalty. $268 per metric tCO2e over the cap, annually. A 100,000 sf office that misses the 2024 cap by even 10% pays roughly $22,000/year. The penalty repeats every year. It is not a one-time fee.

The reporting deadline. First annual reports were due May 1, 2025 (for 2024 emissions), filed via the LL84/LL97 portal. Late or missing reports = $0.50/sf/month penalty on top of the cap penalty.

The compliance pathways. §28-320.3.1 is the standard cap. §28-320.3.2 is the prescriptive pathway for rent-regulated buildings (35%+ regulated units). §28-320.3.3 covers buildings used more than 6 hours/day by 250+ people. §28-320.3.4 is the alternative compliance period for good-faith effort.

This calculator is informational, not legal advice.

Caps and penalties are reproduced from primary text. Mixed-use buildings, rent-regulated allocations, REC offsets, and alternative compliance periods can move your actual obligation. Verify with a registered design professional or LL97 consultant before filing.
