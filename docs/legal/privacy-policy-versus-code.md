# Privacy Policy Versus The Code

For the founder's attorney. Written 2026-10-09 (lane PROTECT-SERVER) by reading `marketing/privacy.html` (last updated October 4, 2026) against the code on branch `claude/protect-server`. Not legal advice.

Nothing in `marketing/privacy.html` or `marketing/terms.html` was edited. A change to either page must also change its version and fingerprint in `utils/legalAcceptanceCore.ts` (`PRIVACY_VERSION`, `PRIVACY_TEXT_SHA256`, `TERMS_VERSION`, `TERMS_TEXT_SHA256`); `scripts/validate-legal-acceptance.ts` fails the build otherwise.

Each row: what the policy says, what the code does, and a plain sentence that would be true. "Missing" means the policy is silent.

## Things The Code Does That The Policy Does Not Mention

| # | What the code does | Where | A sentence that would be true |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Worker identity documents.** A contractor can photograph a crew member's ID. The photo is sent to Google Gemini to read it (`supabase/functions/scan-credential`). By default the image is then discarded and only the ID type, the last four characters and the expiry are stored (`app/crew.tsx:291`, "extract-then-purge"; `supabase/migrations/20260708130000_crew_members.sql:23-37`). If the contractor switches on "retain", the image is stored in the private `worker-ids` bucket (`app/crew.tsx:469-471`, `utils/storage.ts` `uploadWorkerIdImage`). Consent is a box the CONTRACTOR ticks, "I have this person's consent to scan and store their ID information." (`app/crew.tsx:1055`). The worker sees no notice. No retention period is set for a kept image. | as cited | "If you scan a worker's ID, the photo is sent to Google (Gemini) to read it. We store the ID type, the last four characters and the expiry date. We store the photo only if you choose to keep it. You must have the worker's permission first." Counsel to decide whether the worker must be shown a notice directly, and a retention period. |
| 10 | **People who are not users.** The app holds names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, signatures and photos of clients, homeowners, subcontractors and workers who never agreed to anything. The policy speaks only to "you". | portal tables, `crew_members`, `contacts`, `change_order_approvals`, `lien_waivers` | A section "If You Are A Client, Subcontractor Or Worker": what is held about them, that the contractor put it there, and how to ask for a copy or removal. |
| 11 | **Signatures and the signer's user agent.** A client's portal approval stores the typed name, the drawn signature image, the consent text, a document fingerprint and the browser's user agent (`change_order_approvals.user_agent`, `supabase/schema.sql:452-472`). No IP address is stored anywhere. | as cited | "When you sign in a portal we store your name, your signature, the time, a fingerprint of the document and your browser type. We do not store your IP address." |
| 12 | **Room scans stay on the phone.** A saved scan and Apple's raw scan data are written only to the phone's local storage (`utils/roomScan/store.ts:18-51`; keys in `utils/roomScan/storeCore.ts:4-6`). Nothing is uploaded, and they are wiped at sign-out. The feature is owner-preview only (`constants/featureFlags.ts`, `SCAN_ROOM_ENABLED = false`). | as cited | When the feature opens: "A room scan is stored on your phone only. It is not uploaded." Add before the flag is turned on, and update the App Store privacy answers. |
| 13 | **Homeowner project postings.** Photos and drawings a homeowner uploads with a posting were in a public file bucket and each had a permanent public link (`utils/storage.ts`, before this branch). This branch signs links at read time and holds a migration that makes the bucket private (`supabase/migrations/20261010120000_rfp_attachments_private.sql`). Until that migration is applied, the files are public. A posting's row (title, scope, city) is readable by every signed-in account (`supabase/schema.sql:3438`). | as cited | "When you post a project, signed-in contractors can see its title, scope, city, photos and drawings while it is open. Your street address and email are shown only to you." (The second sentence is true only after the held column migration is applied.) |
| 14 | **Acceptance records.** From this branch: a row per acceptance of the Terms and the Privacy Policy and per acknowledged notice, kept after account deletion with the account id removed. | `supabase/migrations/20261010100000_legal_acceptances.sql` | "We keep a record of when you accepted these terms and which version. We keep it after your account is deleted, without your account ID." |
| 15 | **AI request log.** One row per AI request: which feature, which model, sizes, cost, outcome and the account id. No prompt or answer text. 400 days. | `supabase/migrations/20261005090000_ai_call_log.sql:9-49` | "We log each AI request (feature, model, size and cost, not the content) for 400 days." |
| 16 | **The marketing site sets an analytics cookie** with no notice. | `marketing/index.html:50-55`, `persistence: 'localStorage+cookie'` | A cookie sentence in the policy and, if counsel says so, a notice on the site. |
| 17 | **AI on the web has no consent step.** The policy says "In the phone app, AI features send nothing until you allow them" (`privacy.html:101`), which is accurate and silent about the web app, where the question is not asked. | `utils/aiConsentCore.ts:19-20` | Either ask on the web, or say "On the web app, AI features run when you use them." |
| 18 | **Who operates the service.** The policy names "MAGE ID" with no company type, no postal address. | `privacy.html:63` | Company name and address, once the entity is settled. |

## Checked And Found Accurate

- GPS on a jobsite photo, a field-verified takeoff row and a signed field ticket, only on a tap (`utils/photoGeoStamp.ts`, `components/TakeoffFieldVerifyButton.tsx`, `app/field-ticket.tsx`). Matches `privacy.html:70, 81`.
- Sentry: IP collection off, replays for every error session and 10% of others (`app/_layout.tsx:153, 163-164`). Matches `privacy.html:90-93`.
- Google Gemini for most AI features, Anthropic for construction answers and Enterprise takeoff, Rork for speech to text (`supabase/functions/*`; Anthropic appears only in `construction-answer`, `analyze-takeoff` and `_shared`). Matches `privacy.html:97-99`.
- Work logged on another contractor's job is handed to that job's owner (`delete-account`, step 2-0). Matches `privacy.html:148` apart from the GPS words in row 1.
- Only the last four digits of a tax ID are stored (`types/index.ts:2814`, `taxIdLast4`).
