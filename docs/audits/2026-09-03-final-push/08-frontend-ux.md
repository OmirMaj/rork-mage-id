### F1 — [P1] [CONFIRMED] Home and Summary "TODAY ON SITE" run one working day ahead of the schedule everywhere west of Greenwich, and the daily-report "Day N of M" hero is off the same way
- Where: `app/(tabs)/(home)/index.tsx:373-376` (+ `todayScheduleDayNumber` ~:80-100), `app/(tabs)/summary/index.tsx:97-99` (+ `:44-63`), `app/daily-report.tsx:265-275` and `:381-385`; contrast `components/schedule/mobile/MobileScheduleScreen.tsx:180` and `components/schedule/SchedulerHeader.tsx:57-78` (already fixed), `types/index.ts:875` (`startDate` is a bare `YYYY-MM-DD`).
- Evidence: `const baseIso = sched.startDate || p.createdAt; const baseMs = Date.parse(baseIso);` then `todayScheduleDayNumber(baseMs, now, …)` does `const base = new Date(baseMs); base.setHours(0, 0, 0, 0);` — `Date.parse('2026-09-01')` is UTC midnight = Aug 31 18:00 in Denver, so `base` becomes **Aug 31** local. The mobile schedule uses `parseCalendarDay(startDate)` (local midnight Sep 1). daily-report: `const startMs = Date.parse(start); … Math.floor(elapsedMs / ONE_DAY) + 1` — UTC anchor plus raw calendar days on a working-day `totalDurationDays`.
- Failure scenario: iPhone 15 in Denver, dark or light, a 5-day-week schedule starting Tue Sep 1 with "Framing" on day 3 and "Drywall" on day 4. On Sep 3 the Schedule tab says day 3 = Framing; Home and Summary compute day 4 and list **Drywall** under "Today on site" while Framing (which ends today) is dropped. The Daily Report hero reads "Day 4 of 40" and, on a Monday, counts the weekend as worked days. The two flagship strips contradict the screen they link to — a first-week tester with one schedule sees it on day one.
- Fix: replace `Date.parse(baseIso)` with `(parseCalendarDay(baseIso) ?? new Date(baseIso)).getTime()` in all three files (Summary's function comment says it is "replicated verbatim" in Home — fix both), and compute the DFR day number with `scheduleEngine.addWorkingDays`/the same `todayScheduleDayNumber` instead of `elapsedMs / ONE_DAY`.
- Effort: S
