# MAGE ID — UX Audit, 2026-08-03

Walkthrough of the running iOS build (simulator `47BCF882`, dev client on Metro
8081), signed in against real data: 3 projects, ~$121K of budget, 6 daily
reports, 3 change orders, 1 invoice, 20 schedule tasks.

**Method.** Drove the app with `idb` (taps, swipes, text entry) and read the
accessibility tree plus screenshots at each stop. Every finding below is backed
either by a screen I actually reached or by a `file:line` I read. Where I could
not reach a screen, I say so rather than guessing.

**Coverage.** 26 distinct routes reached and inspected by driving, plus ~12 more
verified in source only. `utils/featureRegistry.ts` lists 103 destinations, so
this is roughly a quarter of the surface — weighted deliberately toward the
money and field workflows a contractor runs daily, and toward today's new work.

Reached: `(tabs)/(home)`, `(tabs)/discover`, `discover/tools`, `discover/estimate`,
universal search, `project-detail`, `field-ticket` (picker → list → form →
signature sheet), `cost-seed`, `delay-events` (+ log-a-delay sheet),
`budget-dashboard`, `invoice`, `job-costing`, `schedule-pro`, `daily-report`,
`punch-list`, `rfi`, `client-portal-setup`, `closeout-binder`, `track-record`,
`cost-database`, `business`, `portfolio-margin`, `home-passport`.

Not reached by driving (verified in source where noted): `estimate-wizard`,
`takeoff`, `plan-viewer`, `plans`, `safety`, `summary` tab, `settings` tab,
`change-order` detail, `schedule` lookahead, `widget-setup`, `cost-xray`.

**One environmental note, not a product defect:** a `Render Error` from
`expo-linking` appeared twice mid-session. It is caused by cold-launching the
dev client with `simctl launch` (no Metro manifest). Relaunching via
`…://expo-development-client/?url=http%3A%2F%2Flocalhost%3A8081` fixes it. Also,
`components/BrandSplash.tsx` was being edited concurrently during this audit;
the oversized unstyled splash I saw is that work in progress, not a finding.

## The top 10, ranked by impact on a contractor running a job

### 1. "Bulk Savings" is a green dollar figure the app never computes — and it prints in the client PDF — **broken**

On `project-detail` for The Henderson Residence the hero reads
**Bulk Savings $14,780** against a $44,325 estimate — a 33% saving presented as
fact, in success green, with its own tap-through detail panel titled
"Total Bulk Savings" and an explainer headed "How Bulk Savings Work".

`bulkSavingsTotal` is declared **required** at `types/index.ts:74`. Its only
writers in the entire repo are seed files:

- `utils/demoSeed.ts:136` → `6_200`
- `utils/demoSeed.ts:264` → `14_780`  ← exactly the figure on screen
- `utils/demoSeed.ts:379` → `286_400`
- `app/dev-seeder.tsx:246` → `38_600`
- `app/dev-flagship-seeder.tsx:201` → `74_500`

There is **no code path anywhere that derives it from real data** — not in
`estimate-wizard.tsx`, not in `takeoff.tsx`, not in any context. It is read and
rendered in six places in `app/project-detail.tsx` (`:1109`, `:1159`, `:1162`,
`:1197-1198`, `:1220`, `:1444-1446`, `:2401-2404`).

The damaging part: it goes out to the homeowner. `utils/pdfGenerator.ts:521`
emits it into the client-facing estimate PDF **unguarded**, while the two
adjacent rows *are* guarded:

```
<div class="summary-row savings"><span>Bulk Savings</span><span>-${formatCurrency(legacyEst.bulkSavingsTotal)}</span></div>
${legacyEst.pricePerSqFt > 0 ? `…Price per Sq Ft…` : ''}
${legacyEst.estimatedDuration ? `…Est. Duration…` : ''}
```

Same again in the text export at `:1535`, and
`components/PDFPreSendSheet.tsx:108` ships a "Bulk Savings Breakdown" PDF
section **enabled by default**. So a real contractor's first client proposal
carries a line reading `Bulk Savings  -$0.00`, and the demo data currently
claims $14,780 of savings that nothing measured.

This is the single clearest surviving instance of the class the founder has
spent two days eliminating.

### 2. Payment Predictions renders invented dollars and percentages as fact — **broken**

`app/payment-predictions.tsx:294` shows `{pred.onTimeProbability}%` under the
label "On-time"; `:223` a `collectionRiskScore`; `:253` an `atRiskAmount` in
dollars; `:241/245/249` "Next 7/14/30 days" inflows.

These are raw LLM outputs with Zod fallbacks at `utils/paymentPrediction.ts:36-44`:
`onTimeProbability: z.number().default(50)`, `daysToPay: .default(21)`,
`collectionRiskScore: .default(50)`. A model that omits a field silently renders
**"50%"** and a **"50"** risk score — indistinguishable from a measured result.

Worse, the intro card at `:175` asserts *"MAGE Brain analyzes due dates, client
payment history, project status, and retention holds to forecast real inflows"*
— and that card unmounts the moment results appear (`!result && !loading`). The
results view carries **no caveat at all**. A contractor would make collections
calls on these numbers.

The fix is cheap and the app already knows how to do it — see #10.

### 3. Seven core screens dead-end when opened without a `projectId`, and the app has two competing patterns for it — **broken / confusing**

Opened from universal search (which always arrives with no params), these render
an instructional card and bounce the user back to the Projects tab:

| Route | Headline shown |
|---|---|
| `invoice` | "No invoice open yet" |
| `job-costing` | "No project to cost yet" |
| `daily-report` | "No daily report open yet" |
| `punch-list` | "No punch list open yet" |
| `rfi` | "No RFI open yet" |
| `client-portal-setup` | "No client portal set up yet" |
| `closeout-binder` | "No closeout to deliver yet" |

The user has **three in-progress projects**. "No project to cost yet" is simply
false. Meanwhile `field-ticket` and `budget-dashboard` — reached the same way —
correctly render **"Pick a project"** with the three projects listed, and you're
working in two taps.

So the app ships two answers to the same question, and the worse one is on the
higher-traffic money screens.

`schedule-pro` is the worst of the set and a true dead end
(`app/schedule-pro.tsx:1594-1606`): "No project selected — Open a project first,
then return to Schedule Pro from the header," with a single **"Go back"** button.
No picker, no forward path, and "from the header" doesn't describe anything the
user can see.

This also **contradicts the registry's own stated inclusion rule**
(`utils/featureRegistry.ts:18-21`): *"a destination must stand on its own when
pushed with no params. Screens that dead-end without a projectId … are
deliberately NOT listed here."* All seven above, plus `schedule-pro`, are listed.

### 4. Every internal deep link is dead — **broken**

`utils/deepLinkScheme.ts:38-57`:

```ts
export const PUBLIC_PATHS = new Set<string>(['prequal-form', 'reset-password']);
export function resolveDeepLinkPath(path: string): string {
  const cleaned = stripAppScheme(path);
  const [route] = cleaned.split('?');
  if (route && PUBLIC_PATHS.has(route)) return '/' + cleaned;
  return '/';                                   // ← everything else
}
```

`app/+native-intent.tsx` routes every inbound system path through this. I
confirmed it live: `mageid://cost-seed`, `mageid://delay-events` and
`mageid://field-ticket` all landed on the home screen.

Consequence: a push notification that should open a specific change order, RFI,
or daily report dumps the user on Home with no context. It also breaks links the
app itself constructs — `app/client-portal-setup.tsx:49`
(`${PRIMARY_SCHEME}client-view`), `app/integrations/qbo/callback.tsx:84,98`
(`qbo-setup`), and `utils/crewScan.ts:43` (`claim-crew?token=…`) all resolve to
`/`. The QBO OAuth callback and the crew-claim QR flow cannot complete.

Only `prequal-form` and `reset-password` survive.

*Update, same day:* `PUBLIC_PATHS` was extended during this audit to add
`discover/estimate` and `schedule`. That unblocks two specific screens but
leaves the model intact — every one of the other ~99 destinations still resolves
to `/`. Growing a hand-maintained whitelist one route at a time is the symptom,
not the fix: the gate should distinguish *pre-login public* paths from
*authenticated in-app* paths, and let the latter through to the auth gate rather
than silently rewriting them to Home.

### 5. `delay-events` silently shows one project while presenting itself as the whole register — **confusing**

`app/delay-events.tsx:135`:

```ts
const projectId = params.projectId ?? projects[0]?.id ?? '';
```

On screen the eyebrow read **"HOUSTON PHONE BOOTH AD"** and the hero copy read
*"Every delay, dated, evidenced and notice-tracked."* There is **no project
switcher anywhere in the file** — the only chip rows are cause chips (`:538`),
evidence chips (`:667`) and notice-period chips (`:840`).

So a user who opens "Delay Register" from search or the sidebar sees one
project's delays and is told it's the register. On a claim-defence feature,
where the whole value proposition is completeness of the record, silently
scoping to `projects[0]` is the worst possible failure mode.

Same class, lower severity: `app/cost-xray.tsx:117` has a switcher but gates it
`projects.length > 1 && !hasReviews`, so it **disappears once a scan has
results** — you cannot reassign a scan run against the wrong project.

### 6. Home Passport invents a "home" out of a fallback string — **broken**

`home-passport` rendered a passport titled **"United States"**, stamped
*"On record since 2026 · 0 completed projects · 0 contractors"*, containing two
unrelated jobs under "WORK DONE ON THIS HOME": *Houston Phone Booth Ad* and
*Watermark 9F*. The address toggle offered "124 Park Slope, Brooklyn NY 11215" —
which is The Henderson Residence's address, and Henderson was **not** in the list.

Cause: `utils/passport/consumerPassport.ts:462-465` groups by project location
("Most-frequent project location wins"), and the new-project paths default that
field to the literal string `'United States'` —
`utils/copilot/newProject/newProjectCapability.ts:105` and
`components/UniversalMicButton.tsx:323`. Any two projects created without a
location collapse into one fictional address.

The screen states *"This record belongs to you. It stays with the house no
matter who does the next job."* That claim is being made about a house that does
not exist.

### 7. Today's three new screens are missing from the Tools grid — **confusing**

`cost-seed`, `field-ticket` and `delay-events` are all in `featureRegistry.ts`
but **none appear in `app/(tabs)/discover/tools.tsx`** — I read the full grid
live (AI HUB / DECISIONS / FIELD / MONEY / COMPLIANCE / CLOSEOUT / REPORTING /
NETWORK) and they are absent. FIELD lists Last Planner, Daily reports, Photo
triage, Punch list, Selections, Time tracking, Plans & drawings, Safety — no

They are in `components/DesktopSidebar.tsx` (`:68`, `:112`, `:64`) — but
`utils/useResponsiveLayout.ts:66` sets `showSidebar: isDesktop`, requiring
≥1024pt native. **The sidebar never renders on iOS**, the primary target. So on
the platform that matters, the sidebar is not navigation.

`cost-seed` and `field-ticket` survive via other in-app entry points
(`settings`, `cost-database`, `estimate-wizard`; `project-detail`,
`daily-report`). `delay-events` on mobile is reachable only from inside a daily
report or Schedule Pro.

Six further registry routes are **sidebar-only, i.e. search-only on iOS**:
`(tabs)/marketplace`, `(tabs)/equipment`, `home-passport`, `waiting-on`,
`widget-setup`, `auto-bids`.

Four are **hard orphans with zero inbound taps on any platform**: `sub-profile`
(only `app/_layout.tsx:634`), `profit-leak-history` (`_layout.tsx:1267`),
`integrations`, and `(tabs)/materials` (tile deliberately deleted —
`app/(tabs)/discover/index.tsx:113-115`). `integrations` is the notable one: the
registry advertises it under "QuickBooks & Integrations" with synonyms
`quickbooks`/`qbo`/`accounting`, but Settings routes to `/qbo-setup`
(`app/(tabs)/settings/index.tsx:1067`) — so the screen users *find* is not the
screen the product *uses*.

### 8. More numbers presented as measured that aren't — **broken**

Beyond #1 and #2, in descending order of harm:

- **`app/plan-intelligence.tsx:359,364`** — `{formatMoney(Math.round(r.sqft) * r.ratePerSqft)}`. With no learned rate this uses `DEFAULT_ROOM_RATES` from `utils/planIntelligence.ts:33-37` (kitchen 250, bath 300, garage 45, other 85 — invented). The UI has only *positive* chips (`auto-corrected`, `your rate`, `:367-370`); cold-start rows render a hard dollar total with **no chip**. Absence of a badge is not a disclosure.
- **`app/drawing-analyzer.tsx:503,508`** — `15-30%` "more scope captured" and `2x` "output budget", stated as measured deltas **in an upsell to the Business tier**. Paid-conversion claims are the highest-liability class here.
- **`app/cost-xray.tsx:562`** — `{r.tell.likelihood}% likely to need work`. The trailing clause caveats the *price* but not the *percentage*; `likelihood` is a generic per-tell base rate identical for every user, yet reads as job-specific.
- **`app/estimate-wizard.tsx:679`** — `confidence={result.confidence ?? 70}` renders via `components/brain/BrainCard.tsx:70` as `{conf}% confident` **plus a filled progress bar**. The `?? 70` fallback makes "the model said nothing" look identical to an earned score.
- **`app/estimate-wizard.tsx:122`** — line item hardcoded `name: 'Contingency (~10%)'` while its value is whatever the AI returned. The label asserts a percentage the number may not match, and it propagates to the client PDF.
- Uncited "Industry data" statistics in monetisable surfaces: `estimate-wizard.tsx:924` ("Receive 3 bids in 48h… 35% faster"), `invoice.tsx:1459` ("close 20% more deals at 5-7x larger ticket"), `aia-pay-app.tsx:773` ("Owners average 60-83 days"), `(tabs)/discover/index.tsx:499` ("$25K-$80K loans… 24-hour decisions").
- **`components/BidHitScoreboard.tsx:25,109-110`** — `BENCHMARK_WIN_RATE = 0.25` rendered as "Above the ~25% industry average". The user's own win rate is real; the benchmark is invented. Also ungated on sample size, so one won bid renders "100%".

### 10. Design-system drift the user can see — **polish**

Full data in the drift audit; the parts a user actually perceives:

- **`fontWeight: '800'` is used 882 times app-wide** (plus 15 × `'900'`). It is not in the sanctioned `Weight` union in `constants/typography.ts`, whose own comment reads *"Boldness is currency; we'd spent it all."* Worst: `takeoff.tsx` (20), `estimate-wizard.tsx` (17), `field-ticket.tsx` (15), `closeout-binder.tsx` (13).
- **`Type` is half-adopted.** Screens write `fontSize: Type.footnote.fontSize` instead of `...Type.footnote`, taking the size but **dropping `lineHeight` and `letterSpacing`**. Cherry-pick counts: `daily-report` 90, `takeoff` 65, `invoice` 54, `estimate-wizard` 54, `settings` 50, `change-order` 49, `field-ticket` 44, `punch-list` 43. This is the root cause of most of the rest.
- **One semantic role, seven sizes.** "Section heading" resolves as 14/700 (`job-costing.tsx:794`), 15/700 (`daily-report.tsx:3014`, `field-ticket.tsx:1379`), 16/**800** (`takeoff.tsx:2115`), 17/700 (`client-portal-setup.tsx:1291`), 18/700 (`cost-seed.tsx:682`), serif 22 (`estimate-wizard.tsx:1490`).
- **Card rhythm breaks in a single scroll.** On the home screen, `statCard` is `padding: 18` (`(tabs)/(home)/index.tsx:1299`) while `RecoveredCard`/`DailyLogCard` directly beneath it use `Tokens.spacing.md` (16) — two paddings 2pt apart, stacked. The same screen uses **four** card radii (16, 24, 14, 12) plus an inline `borderRadius: 14` at `:912`; the token doc asks for two per surface.
- **`Tokens.shadow.*` is used twice in the whole repo.** 75 files hand-roll shadows, producing 19 distinct `shadowOpacity` values against a 3-tier token.
- **Hardcoded dark-theme hexes** in the `estimate-wizard` hero (`:1230-1235`, `:1473`, `:1487` — `#FF8533`, `#F4EFE6`, `#C9C3B8`, `#0F1216`, `#E8E5DD`; the last two exist nowhere in the token file). That card cannot follow the theme. Its hero total is `fontSize: 46` (`:1233`) against a serif scale that tops out at 36.

Worst → cleanest: `takeoff` (123) · `estimate-wizard` (121) · `daily-report`
(117) · `closeout-binder` (68) · `settings` (56) · `client-portal-setup` (55) ·

## Smaller findings

- **`field-ticket` computes `unpricedRowCount` and throws it away.** `utils/fieldTicketCore.ts:123` returns it; `app/field-ticket.tsx` never renders it. I added a labour row with no rate and the screen showed **"Ticket total $0.00"** and a button reading **"Sign & seal · $0.00"**. Note the *underlying design is right and deliberate* — `fieldTicketCore.ts:12-16` argues a super signs for work, not money, and the signature sheet says so explicitly ("Pricing is billed under the contract's T&M rates"). The defect is narrower: a literal `$0.00` on the seal button, when the screen already knows the number is "1 unpriced line", not "zero dollars". **Polish.**
- **`area-takeoff` is a functional dead end from its own nav entries.** `app/area-takeoff.tsx:133` — no project picker in the file. Opened with no params (which is how `DesktopSidebar.tsx:69` and `components/summary/ToolsSheet.tsx` push it), the tool fully works but `canAddToEstimate` (`:147`) is permanently false, so the measurement has nowhere to go. `:668` admits it. **Confusing.**
- **Tab bar reports "1 of 12", "2 of 12", "4 of 12", "5 of 12"** to VoiceOver — index 3 is skipped, so the accessible tab count doesn't match what's rendered. **Polish.**
- **Home's headline CO figure is dominated by trivia.** "$287 in change orders ready to send" (CO #3 $250, CO #2 $37) against a $121K portfolio. Real data, correctly summed — but the card spends the most valuable slot on the smallest number in the app. Worth a floor, or ranking by value. **Polish.**

## What works — and it is a lot

**`delay-events` is the best screen in the app.** It is also the reference
implementation for the design system: 18 `...Type` spreads, zero raw
`fontSize`, zero off-scale weights, zero hex literals, zero inline shadows
(drift score **3**, versus 123 for `takeoff`). Migrate other screens toward it.

Its copy is the standard the rest of the product should be held to:

> "MAGE assembles and narrates. It does not decide entitlement, and recording a
> notice here is not the same act as serving it under your contract."

> *Date you first knew* — "This starts the notice clock — not the day you got
> round to typing it up."

> *Days another delay ran concurrently* — "Optional, and worth being honest
> about. When your own delay overlaps the owner's, the usual US rule gives you
> the time but not the money — a register that can't show it gets taken apart in
> one question."

That last field is a genuine competitive moat. No SMB competitor models
concurrent delay, and the explanation teaches the contractor something they can
use in a negotiation.

**`cost-seed` solves the cold-start problem honestly.** "MAGE normally learns
your rates from jobs you close here — which means nothing to price from on day
one." And critically: *"Rates you enter are marked 'you set this' — never
counted as a closed job, never folded into your bid accuracy. The first real job
you close corrects them."* That is provenance discipline most mature products
never achieve.

**`business` and `track-record` are the model for honest metrics.** Everything
null-gates instead of defaulting: "CRM Win Rate **—** / 2 more decided leads
needed", "3 decided bids needed", "0 of 2 jobs needed for your first type
comparison", "Still learning", "Nothing graded yet". `business` even publishes
its definitions inline — *"Margin = (contract − committed cost) ÷ contract.
Excludes approved change orders from cost — same definition used in Bid
Advisor"* — and discloses its own modelling assumption for crew load. This is
exactly what #2, #8 need.

**The weather-provenance fix held.** I verified it end to end: every
`DayForecast` carries a required `source: 'live' | 'simulated'`
(`utils/weatherService.ts:43`), and **all 8** render sites mount
`SimulatedWeatherBanner` / `SimulatedDayChip` — including the schedule lookahead
(`app/(tabs)/schedule/index.tsx:3387,3397`), `TodayView.tsx:482,489,501` and
`GanttChart.tsx:169`. Copy is centralised in `utils/weatherProvenance.ts`, and
`WeatherRescheduleModal` refuses to write simulated days to the delay log. **No
uncaveated weather display exists.**

**Superseded drawings are handled properly.** `app/plans.tsx:68-78` hides
superseded sheets by default with a "Show N superseded" pill and keeps them for
audit history; `app/plan-viewer.tsx:474` carries a dedicated banner. The comment
at `plans.tsx:294` notes the badge is unconditional on `superseded`, not gated
on revision — the right call.

**The CO→schedule reflow is wired from both approve paths**, which I expected to
be a gap and wasn't: `app/project-detail.tsx:4430` *and*
`app/change-order.tsx:1191` both mount `COScheduleReflowPreviewModal`. When a CO
is approved but its days aren't yet placed, `change-order.tsx:771-772` says so
and names the next step.

**`field-ticket` is a strong feature.** Progressive validation that names the
single next blocker ("Still need: Description of the work performed" → "…At
least one labor, material, or equipment line"), trade chips, a real signature
sheet with a correct attestation, and a data-layer seal
(`fieldTicketCore.ts:18-27`) that freezes content once signed and blocks
double-conversion to a CO via an `auditTrail` marker that survives a cache wipe.

**Best-in-app empty states** — copy these: `field-ticket` ("Extra work you never
got signed for is the money you lose at closeout…" + **New T&M ticket** button)
and `cost-database` (three numbered steps + a **Seed your rates** button that
goes straight to `cost-seed`).

**`DailyLogCard` on home earns its place.** "1 job has no log for today," then
the reason it matters: *"A day with no log at all is the gap that gets the whole
record attacked, and filing it late does not close it."* Per-project rows read
"2 of 21 working days logged" — a real ratio, not a score.

**`RecoveredCard` correctly renders nothing.** It didn't appear on home, and
that is right: `utils/recoveredValue.ts` counts only change orders that are
**approved and signed by the client**, and the card renders nothing at $0
(`components/home/RecoveredCard.tsx:72-76`). Both cards are also design-clean
(drift score 2 each).

**`project-detail` is genuinely good** — tile grid with live counts (5 daily
reports, 15 estimate items, 3 COs, 1 invoice, 20 schedule tasks), a 4-stage
progress rail, and honest labelling on the estimate card
("Bid confidence — building from your jobs").

**`components/AIBidScorecard.tsx:303-310` is the pattern to copy** for #2 and #8:
win probability null-gates to *"Not enough decided bids to estimate odds —
tracked from your next wins/losses"* with a "{n} decided bids tracked so far"
row.

## Is this ready for a first user?

**Close, but not this week — and the gap is small and specific.**

The product substance is there. The claim-defence pillar, the cost-learning
loop, the T&M ticket, and the metric honesty in `business`/`track-record` are
better than what most SMB construction tools ship, and the writing is far
better. Nothing I hit crashed. The two hardest things to retrofit — a coherent
domain model and a voice — are already done.

Four things should block a first user, and all four are days of work, not weeks:

1. **Delete or compute `bulkSavingsTotal` (#1).** It is a fabricated saving that
   prints in the client's PDF by default. Given nothing computes it, deleting
   the field and its six render sites is the honest fix.
2. **Null-gate the prediction defaults (#2, #8).** Change `.default(50)` /
   `?? 70` to `null` and render "—" plus a "needs N more" line. `AIBidScorecard`
   already does exactly this; it is a pattern copy, not new design.
3. **Give the seven project-scoped screens the picker that `field-ticket`
   already has (#3).** One shared component, seven call sites. Today a user who
   searches "invoices" — with three projects and an unpaid invoice — is told
   they have none.
4. **Fix `resolveDeepLinkPath` (#4).** It silently breaks QBO OAuth, crew-claim
   QR, the client portal link, and every push notification. This will look like
   random flakiness in the field and be very hard to diagnose from a user report.

Then #5 (`delay-events` project scoping) and #6 (Home Passport) before anyone
leans on those two features, and #7 so today's new work is reachable on iOS
without typing its name.
