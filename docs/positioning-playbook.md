# Positioning Playbook — why a GC chooses MAGE ID over anyone else

Date: 2026-06-08
Source: the 2026 competitive landscape sweep (`docs/competitive-landscape-2026.md`).
Purpose: the single source of truth for messaging — paywall, app store, marketing site, ads, sales. Everything customer-facing should ladder up to the wedge below.

## The wedge (memorize this)

> **Everyone bids. We *learn*. Everyone organizes your job. We *defend your margin*.**

The research proved it across 24+ competitors: **not one** prices from the contractor's *own* job actuals or scores margin risk forward. They bid off generic Home Depot/RSMeans/ZIP data or dead-end at a quantity you paste into Excel. MAGE is the only one whose estimate gets smarter every time you close a job — and the only one that tells you a job is about to lose money *before* it does.

**The category line:**
> **The AI that makes you money — not just organizes your job.**

---

## The three proof pillars (always back the wedge with these)

1. **Your prices, learned from your jobs.** Every closed job teaches MAGE what your scopes *actually* cost (Cost Database). Your next bid is priced from your reality — not a generic catalog that bids $7,500 to paint 8 doors (a real, documented competitor failure).
2. **Margin that defends itself.** Living Estimate, Margin Risk, the Portfolio Board, and Margin Alerts watch every job and warn you the moment one starts to bleed. Nobody else does forward-looking margin.
3. **One connected loop, not eight rented tools.** Estimate → takeoff (priced from your costs) → buyout → commitments → actuals → margin → *sharper next estimate*. The whole thing, on your phone, for a flat price.

## The trust / accuracy stance (critical — don't get lumped with the hype tools)

The "instant AI estimate" tools earned a reputation for being wildly wrong. **Do not out-hype them. Out-*trust* them.**

> "MAGE doesn't guess. It prices from *your* real numbers, shows its confidence, and flags the lines it isn't sure about. Your estimate, your costs — not an AI's imagination."

Lead with **honesty + your own data**, never "magic." This is the counter-position to the skeptics' "AI estimates are unreliable" bucket.
